# Abstract

Ecopath represents trophic flows using per-group mass and energy balances; Ecosim adds temporal biomass dynamics and Ecospace adds spatial structure. The framework supports uncertainty analysis, fitting, forcing, age structure, tracer and nutrient flows, and policy exploration, but balanced inputs and good fits do not guarantee unique parameters, correct mechanisms, equilibrium, or reliable predictions.
