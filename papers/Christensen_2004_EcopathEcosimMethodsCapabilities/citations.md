# Citations and new leads

- Christensen and Pauly (1992), “Ecopath II—a software for balancing steady-state ecosystem models and calculating network characteristics”: the direct precursor for the static balance equations. [PDF p. 30; journal p. 138]
- Walters et al. (1997), “Structuring dynamic models of exploited ecosystems from trophic mass-balance assessments”: the direct dynamic-model bridge from Ecopath to Ecosim. [PDF p. 31; journal p. 139]
- Ulanowicz (1986), *Growth and Development: Ecosystem Phenomenology*: a systems/network treatment cited for ecosystem flow structure. [PDF p. 31; journal p. 139]

These are leads only; no current code claim relies on their unread contents.
