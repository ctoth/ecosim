# Citations and new leads

- Goguen and Burstall, “Introducing Institutions” (1984): the earlier institution presentation cited by the paper. [PDF p. 50; journal p. 143, ref. 44]
- Goguen and Burstall, “A Study in the Foundations of Programming Methodology” (1986): charters and parchments, proposed as constructions that can guarantee institution laws. [PDF p. 50; journal p. 144, ref. 47]
- Tarlecki, “Bits and Pieces of the Theory of Institutions” (1986): further categorical properties of institutions. [PDF p. 52; journal p. 146, ref. 97]
- Mosses, “Unified Algebras and Institutions” (1989): an additional route connecting algebraic semantics and institutions. [PDF p. 51; journal p. 145, ref. 80]

These are leads only; no claim in the current implementation audit depends on their unread contents.
