# Paper-backed implementation audit

## Selection criteria

This audit used only papers already present in the project that directly constrain the implemented Institution semantics, exact conservation derivation, compartment-flow ledger, trace checking, or model-verification claims, and that had complete enough artifacts for page-level verification. No recency, popularity, implementation convenience, or publication-readiness criterion was used.

## Findings

| Source obligation | Implementation result | Remaining boundary |
|---|---|---|
| A Goguen institution has a signature category, a covariant sentence functor, a contravariant model functor, and satisfaction invariant under change of notation. [Goguen and Burstall, PDF pp. 3–6, 39–47](../papers/Goguen_1992_InstitutionsAbstractModelTheory/notes.md) | `institution::Institution` now exposes signature identities and composition. `institution::laws` observes category identity/associativity, sentence identity/composition, model-reduct identity/composition, and the satisfaction square on supplied examples. The conservation adapter implements these operations. | The Rust boundary represents model objects but not homomorphisms in each model category. The helpers test examples rather than prove laws universally. Cross-domain adapters are not institution morphisms/comorphisms until their naturality and satisfaction-transfer laws exist. |
| Structural linear conservation vectors satisfy `omega * Gamma = 0`; their dimension is rows minus rank. Nonlinear and rate-dependent first integrals are additional phenomena. [Mahdi et al., PDF pp. 2–5, 11–20](../papers/Mahdi_2017_ConservationLawsBiochemicalReaction/notes.md) | `conservation-linear` now property-tests arbitrary exact integer matrices: every returned vector annihilates every transition column, basis size equals nullity, and returned vectors are independent. Documentation calls the result a structural linear rational vector-space basis. | Nonlinear/Darboux and kinetic-parameter-specific invariant discovery remains a separate research layer. |
| Ecosystem energy accounts distinguish external input, internal trophic transfer, storage, decomposition, and dissipation; internal transfers cancel globally. [Lindeman, PDF pp. 2–18](../papers/Lindeman_1942_TrophicDynamicAspectEcology/notes.md) | `ecosim-core` has separate input, transfer, and output operations; the compiled food web retains nutrient, producer, consumer, and detritus stocks and distinct growth, grazing, mortality, decomposition, input, and harvest flows. Rust and Python properties exercise internal cancellation and boundary accounting. | The models expose process-level settlement evidence and cumulative boundary totals, but not a durable public typed event ledger. Respiration and dissipation remain outside the deliberately small food-web mechanism set. |
| Balance equations are currency-specific consistency constraints, not proof of equilibrium, unique parameters, correct mechanisms, or predictive validity. [Christensen and Walters, PDF pp. 1–12, 19–29](../papers/Christensen_2004_EcopathEcosimMethodsCapabilities/notes.md) | The foundation ledger explicitly names energy and uses exact units through Bridgman at the Python boundary; the food web uses one clearly documented material-equivalent currency and open-system boundary accounting. | Neither the two-compartment ledger nor four-stock food web establishes empirical adequacy. Generic currencies, uncertainty, forcing, richer functional responses, age/space structure, calibration, and independent validation belong in later layers. |
| Verification checks the implemented formalism; validation requires an intended purpose, performance criteria, and context. Extreme-condition, event, trace, and internal-consistency tests are useful but do not validate an ecosystem. [Rykiel, PDF pp. 1–14](../papers/Rykiel_1996_TestingEcologicalModelsValidation/notes.md) | A Hypothesis rule-based state machine maintains an independent exact oracle over arbitrary large integers, valid operations, rejected overdraws, negative inputs, and self-transfers. Invariants check stock, boundary totals, history length, residual, and the derived law after checkable traces. Rust properties separately check rejected-operation atomicity. | No empirical validation is claimed or attempted. A real ecosystem model must state its purpose, acceptance thresholds, context, data provenance, calibration procedure, and independent evaluation plan. |
| The NPZD benchmark composes an autonomous production-destruction system with analytic nutrient pulses and sinking, and uses a modified Patankar--Runge--Kutta method to preserve positivity and conservation. [Mata Almonacid and Medel, PDF pp. 4–13, 20–25](../papers/Almonacid_2020_Structure-preservingNumericalApproachSimulating/notes.md) | `paper_models::almonacid_2020` independently implements the paper's production matrix, two linear MPRK solves, analytic Gaussian and sinking maps, and input/export ledger. Deterministic and property tests exercise the structural obligations, the Figure 4 sinking-rate sweep, and the three-pulse case. | The Figure 10 value remains a deliberately ignored red acceptance target because of an unresolved source/model reproduction discrepancy: time-step refinement and left/mid/right light sampling converge near 33.7 rather than 24.38. Figure 4 also omits the detritus floor, while its printed pulse separation conflicts with its own formula and parameters. |
| The Heinle and Flemming source was selected to constrain an independent ecosystem benchmark rather than another wrapper around the generic food web. | No equations, parameters, or executable model were authored from incomplete evidence. | Full text was closed at Unpaywall/OpenAlex and the publisher offered only purchase or unspecified institutional access. Implementation waits for a legitimately supplied PDF or named library account. |
